# CRITTERS — AWAKENED CREATURES OF ALABAMA

Three bespoke Awakened creatures for the Alabama corridor, built on canon SR3 *Critters* templates.
Stat blocks use the standard SR3 critter line — **B Q S C I W E R** — where **B** is `Body/Armor`,
**Q** is `Rating × running-multiplier`, and `E` may carry a `Z` (dual-natured) suffix. Powers and
Weaknesses cite the *Critters* sourcebook page; anything not literally in the book is tagged
`[homebrew]`.

---

## The Kudzu Strangler

**Region:** North Alabama — Black Belt prairie, kudzu backroads between Huntsville and Decatur
**Concept:** An Awakened parasitic vine-mass that consumes abandoned infrastructure, spreading through the region's endless kudzu. Patient, opportunistic; uses the kudzu network as a pseudo-limb system.

**Ecology:** It doesn't hunt — it waits. Travelers snared in kudzu thickets are enveloped by accelerated vine growth. Drawn to decay and neglect, it thrives on the region's industrial ruin, moving slowly but able to surge when threatened or hungry. During storms it is nearly invisible among the wild growth.

**Stats:**
```
 B    Q      S   C   I   W   E    R
6/3  2 x 1   6   2   3   5   7    3
INIT: 3 + 1D6
Attacks: 6M (Entangling Grapple / Vine Whip)
```

**Powers:**
- **Engulf** — *Critters* p.27
- **Movement (Self Only)** — *Critters* p.27
- **Concealment** — *Critters* p.37 *(Nature Spirit power — see ruling below)*
- **Binding** — *Critters* p.37 *(Nature Spirit power — see ruling below)*
- **Corrosive Secretion (acidic sap)** — *Critters* p.17 `[homebrew descriptor]`

**Weaknesses:**
- **Allergy (Sunlight, Moderate)** — *Critters* p.42
- **Reduced Sense (sight — relies on vibration/vibration-sensing)** `[homebrew]`

**Based on:** Nature-Spirit power set (*Critters* p.37) grafted onto a slow, high-Body plant critter; Engulf/Movement per p.27.

**GM Notes:**
- *Encounter:* An abandoned Black Belt factory is "reclaimed" overnight — vines move on their own.
- *Threat:* Moderate. Its Binding immobilizes, but it needs time to grow strong enough to kill unaided; dangerous in numbers or when a mana surge accelerates it.
- *Ruling — critter vs. spirit:* Binding and Concealment are canonically **Nature Spirit** powers. For this home campaign the Kudzu Strangler is a **critter** using a fixed stat block (above); its Binding/Concealment operate at an effective Force equal to its **Essence (7)** rather than a summoned Force. It cannot be banished like a spirit — it must be fought or burned out.

## The Slagwyrm (Birmingham Industrial-Decay Critter)

**Region:** Central Alabama — Birmingham's derelict steel mills, slag heaps, and warehouse district
**Concept:** A **toxic critter** born of a century of iron-and-coke pollution: a metal-scaled serpent-thing that nests in cooling furnaces and slag pits, exhaling caustic industrial fumes.

**Ecology:** Grown from the toxic mana of Birmingham's abandoned Sloss-era furnaces, the Slagwyrm is a dual-natured toxic critter. It lairs in slag heaps and dead blast furnaces, feeding on rust, runoff, and the occasional squatter. It is drawn to pollution and industrial ruin the way a normal critter is drawn to clean territory.

**Stats:**
```
 B    Q      S   C   I   W   E    R
5/4  4 x 3   5   2   3   4   6Z   4
INIT: 4 + 1D6
Attacks: 6M (bite) or Noxious Breath
```

**Powers:**
- **Noxious Breath** — *Critters* p.13
- **Immunity (Pathogens, Poisons)** — *Critters* p.27
- **Enhanced Senses (Thermographic Vision)** — *Critters* p.42
- **Concealment (Personal)** — *Critters* p.25 *(as Devil Rat)*
- **Toxic-critter Combat Pool bonus** — *Critters* p.17 *(1 Essence → +1 Combat Pool die)*

**Weaknesses:**
- **Allergy (purified water / purified air, Severe)** — *Critters* p.17 *(the defining toxic-critter weakness)*

**Based on:** SR3 **Toxic Critter** rules (*Critters* p.17) applied to a serpentine body; Noxious Breath p.13, Immunity/Concealment from the Devil Rat template p.25–27.

**GM Notes:**
- *Encounter:* Runners casing a "cold" Birmingham warehouse find the air burning their lungs before they ever see it.
- *Threat:* Moderate-to-serious in its lair (its Noxious Breath fills enclosed spaces); much weaker if lured into clean air or rain, which triggers its Severe allergy.
- *Hook:* A corp wants the slag site cleared for redevelopment and has quietly put a bounty on "the thing in Furnace No. 3."

## The Riverhaunt (Tennessee River Drowned)

**Region:** North Alabama — the Tennessee River, Wheeler/Guntersville reservoirs, flooded bottomland
**Concept:** An aquatic Awakened predator of the dammed Tennessee, a pale eel-formed thing that lures prey into deep flood-water and drowns it, like a New-World *each-uisge*.

**Ecology:** Native to the drowned valleys behind the TVA dams, the Riverhaunt haunts submerged towns and flooded timber. It mimics distress sounds — a crying child, a stranded dog — to draw prey to the water's edge, then drags them under. Local rigger crews on the river trade stories of it fouling props and dragging down swimmers.

**Stats:**
```
 B     Q      S    C   I   W   E    R
7/2  5 x 4   8    2   3   3   6    5
INIT: 5 + 1D6
Attacks: 7S (constrict/drag), +1 Reach
```

**Powers:**
- **Enhanced Senses (Sonar)** — *Critters* p.35 *(as Leviathan)*
- **Mimicry** — *Critters* p.35 *(as Leviathan)*
- **Fear** — *Critters* p.13
- **Binding (aquatic grapple/drag)** — *Critters* p.37 `[homebrew reskin — treated as a critter power at Essence 6]`

**Weaknesses:**
- **Dependency (must submerge in fresh water regularly)** `[homebrew]`
- **Reduced Sense (poor eyesight above water)** `[homebrew]`

**Based on:** Aquatic templates **Leviathan** (Sonar/Mimicry, p.35) and the drowning-predator behavior of the canon **Each-Uisge** (p.27); Fear p.13.

**GM Notes:**
- *Encounter:* A milk-run river crossing turns deadly when a "stranded swimmer" turns out to be bait.
- *Threat:* Serious in deep water (Strength 8 drag, drowns a bound target); minimal on land, where it beaches and flails.
- *Hook:* The TVA and a local fixer both want proof of what's been sinking boats near a flooded ghost town — for very different reasons.
