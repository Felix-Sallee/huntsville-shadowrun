# DEREK VANCE

![Derek Vance](images/derek-vance.jpg)  
*Derek Vance, Head of Internal Affairs at Princeps.*

---

## Background

Derek Vance runs Princeps's internal affairs division — investigations, vetting, and disciplinary action. A former federal investigator with a reputation for digging up secrets that make people disappear, Vance ensures that the Princeps hierarchy stays clean and that any employee who gets too close to a secret is dealt with swiftly.

---

## What the Crew Knows

The crew knows that Derek Vance is in charge of internal affairs at Princeps. They know he's a former federal investigator and that he has a reputation for digging up secrets and dealing with employees who cross lines. Whether they know about Vance's methods — including the elimination of witnesses — is a question for the campaign.

---

## Role in the Campaign

Derek Vance can be used in the campaign in several ways:

- **The Investigator.** The crew can be hired by Vance to investigate an internal leak, a missing employee, or a suspected corporate spy.
- **The Target.** The crew can be investigated by Vance if they're suspected of being involved in an incident, or if they've gotten too close to a secret.
- **The Ally.** The crew can be recruited by Vance for an internal probe, with the risk that the probe goes wrong and the crew has to clean up the fallout.
- **The Enemy.** The crew can be hired by Vance's enemies — rival corporations, the government, or someone trying to undermine Princeps — to sabotage Vance's work.

---

## Appearance and Attributes

**Age:** 43  
**Archetype:** Professional (Investigator)  
**Traits:** Calculating, precise, unyielding  
**Appearance:** Vance is a sharp-dressed man with a sharp gaze and a habit of looking at people as if he's already decided what they're hiding. He dresses in tailored suits and carries a leather portfolio everywhere, always ready with a file on someone's past.
