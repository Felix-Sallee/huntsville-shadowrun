# CHLOE OKAFOR

![Chloe Okafor](images/chloe-okafor.jpg)  
*Chloe Okafor, Chief Data Officer and IT Director at Princeps.*

---

## Background

Chloe Okafor is Princeps's cyber-architect. A former corporate hacker who turned legitimate security architect, Chloe manages the corporation's digital infrastructure, firewall defenses, and covert data operations. Her network spans every corner of Princeps's digital footprint, and she's the only person who can bypass the security protocols that even the security division can't touch.

---

## What the Crew Knows

The crew knows that Chloe Okafor is the Chief Data Officer and IT Director at Princeps. They know she manages the corporation's cyber infrastructure and that she has backdoor access to systems that the rest of the security team doesn't know about. Whether they know about Chloe's former career as a corporate hacker — and whether she's still using old skills — is a question for the campaign.

---

## Role in the Campaign

Chloe Okafor can be used in the campaign in several ways:

- **The Cyber-Target.** The crew can be hired by Chloe to test a new security system, or to hack Princeps's network as a stress test.
- **The Hacker.** The crew can be hired by Chloe's enemies — rival corporations, the government, or someone trying to compromise Princeps — to breach her security.
- **The Ally.** The crew can be recruited by Chloe for a covert data operation, with the risk that the operation goes wrong and the crew has to clean up the fallout.
- **The Enemy.** The crew can be hired by Chloe's enemies to sabotage her work or steal sensitive data.

---

## Appearance and Attributes

**Age:** 35  
**Archetype:** Professional (Datajack)  
**Traits:** Sharp, secretive, resourceful  
**Appearance:** Chloe is a slender woman with dark eyes and a habit of tapping her fingers when she's thinking. She dresses in a mix of corporate formal wear and tech-accessories, often with multiple screens connected to her. She has a habit of looking at screens when people are talking to her.
