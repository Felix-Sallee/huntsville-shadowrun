# DR. ARIS THORNE

![Dr. Aris Thorne — Corporate Photo](images/arise-thorne-CEO.jpg)  
*Dr. Aris Thorne, Chief Scientific Officer and Head of Research & Development at Princeps.*

---

## Background

Dr. Aris Thorne is the brilliant, morally flexible scientist who actually runs the Princeps research program. A former government researcher, Aris joined Princeps to pursue projects that the government would never greenlight. His lab is the heart of Princeps's technological advantage, and he's deeply entangled in the corporation's darkest projects.

---

## What the Crew Knows

The crew knows about Aris's black-site operations, particularly **Black Site 4**, the deniable research facility off the main campus. They know that Aris runs projects that aren't approved for the main campus and that his security detail is separate from Devlin's team. Whether they know the exact nature of Aris's research is a question for the campaign.

---

## Role in the Campaign

Aris Thorne can be used in the campaign in several ways:

- **The Researcher.** The crew can be hired by Aris to assist with his research, protect his lab, or investigate anomalies in his experiments.
- **The Target.** The crew can be hired to infiltrate Black Site 4 and discover Aris's secret project, or to sabotage his research.
- **The Ally.** The crew can be recruited by Aris for a project that could change the geopolitical landscape, with the risk that the project goes wrong and the crew has to clean up the fallout.
- **The Enemy.** The crew can be hired by Aris's enemies — rival corporations, the government, or someone trying to shut down the project — to stop Aris and destroy his work.

---

## Appearance and Attributes

**Age:** 52  
**Archetype:** Scientist (Biochem)  
**Traits:** Brilliant, paranoid, obsessive  
**Appearance:** Aris is a tall, gaunt man with sharp features and tired eyes. He has a habit of rubbing his temples when he's thinking and a tendency to mutter to himself when he's deep in thought. He dresses in a mix of academic formal wear and lab equipment, often with a stethoscope around his neck.
