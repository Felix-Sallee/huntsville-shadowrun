# THE STAR-CAPTIVE

**The Star-Captive** — Free Spirit (Guardian) · **Force 3**

A free spirit whose form resembles a human astronaut in a slightly tattered space suit, drifting in a pale blue glow. Cracks in the helmet visor reveal starlight instead of a face, and the suit hums faintly with residual spirit energy. It seems to have been pulled from some unknown orbital craft and has been wandering the land since.

> *They say it came from the stars. The suit was torn at the seams, the visor cracked open like a cracked eggshell revealing not a face but a swirling void of cold light. It never spoke — only watched. And when it chose to speak, the words arrived not in the air but behind your eyes, soft as a lullaby and strange as a dream. It doesn't want to fight, doesn't want to bind. It just wants to see what happens next. Some days, it leaves a single white feather on your dashboard. Others, it's gone before dawn, and you're left wondering if you imagined it at all.*

**Source:** [homebrew]

**Weakness:** The Star-Captive can be bound if its True Name is spoken three times in succession (MIS p.117), and it may sacrifice Spirit Energy to resist binding attempts.

**Personality:** Shadows (observant, contemplative, drawn to the unknown)

**Spirit Energy:** 1 (can increase to 3, its Force, by receiving Good Karma)

**Powers:**

- **Astral Gateway** — The Star-Captive can use its astral gateway power to bring the astral and physical planes into closer contact (MIS p.119). As long as it maintains watch over a traveler's physical body, the traveler suffers no Essence loss.
- **Human Form** — The Star-Captive appears as a human astronaut with a faint ethereal glow (MIS p.119).
- **Aura Masking** — The Star-Captive can mask its aura to match its astronaut appearance or mask it entirely from astral perception (MIS p.119).
- **Possession** — The Star-Captive can inhabit a willing subject to move through the physical world more effectively (MIS p.119).

**Attack Roll Pool:** 3 + Sorcery (derived from Force + Spirit Energy, per MIS p.116)

**Physical Attributes:** Based on a total Force of 3 + Spirit Energy, at minimum 3 (MIS p.113)

---

**Design rationale:**

- **Guardian type** — A benevolent, observant spirit that protects/guides rather than fights. Fits the "astronaut" concept as a gentle explorer (MIS p.114).
- **Human Form + ghostly astronaut visual** — Free Spirits can take human form; the astronaut aesthetic is a visual flavor of its materialized appearance (MIS p.119).
- **Power set** — All four powers come from the standard Free Spirit Powers table (MIS p.122): Telepathy is a standard skill (SR3E Core p.179) and not a spirit power, so it was replaced with canonical powers. Aura Masking replaces the old "Spirit Sight" label.
- **Force & personality** — Force 3 is low, matching the "Shadows" personality type (contemplative, drawn to the unknown). It's an explorer spirit, not a warrior.
- **Spirit Energy** — Fixed at 1 (can grow to 3 with Good Karma), matching MIS p.116 rules.
