# THE STAR-CAPTIVE

**Free Spirit** (Spirit of the Sky base) · **Force 3** · **Motivation: Guardian** · **Spirit Energy 1** · **Source:** `[homebrew]`

A free spirit that wears the shape of a human astronaut in a slit, sun-bleached pressure suit, drifting a hand's breadth above the ground inside a pale-blue corona. The visor is cracked open like a broken eggshell, and behind it there is no face — only a slow churn of cold starlight. It does not breathe, does not speak aloud; when it chooses to be understood, the words simply arrive behind your eyes, soft and unhurried. It came down out of the Huntsville sky some years back, and it has been walking the Tennessee Valley ever since, watching.

> *They say it came from the stars. The suit was torn at the seams, the visor cracked open like an eggshell — and behind it, not a face but a swirling void of cold light. It never spoke; it only watched. And when it chose to speak, the words arrived behind your eyes, soft as a lullaby and strange as a dream. It doesn't want to fight. It doesn't want to bind you. It wants to see what happens next. Some mornings it leaves a single white feather on your dashboard. Other mornings it's simply gone before dawn, and you spend the drive to work wondering whether you dreamed the whole thing.*

The Star-Captive is a **Guardian**: it is drawn to the unspoiled land around the old NASA fields and the river bottoms, and it despises the corps that would strip-mine or pave them. It rarely has much use for metahumans — but it has, on occasion, quietly steered a runner team toward derailing a corporate plan against the valley's wild places. It reads as gentle. It is not harmless.

## Stat Block

Built on the SR3 **Spirit of the Sky** template *(SR3 p.266)*, reskinned as an orbital/void spirit `[homebrew]`. Physical Attributes are derived from an **effective Force of 4** — its Force 3 **plus** its Spirit Energy of 1, per the free-spirit rule that Spirit Energy is added to Force for the spirit's physical form *(Magic in the Shadows p.116)*.

## Powers

- **Astral Gateway** *(MIS p.119)* — brings the astral and physical planes into closer contact. While the Star-Captive keeps watch over a traveler's abandoned physical body, that astral traveler suffers no Essence loss for extended projection.
- **Aura Masking** *(MIS p.119)* — masks its own aura, appearing mundane (or vanishing entirely) to astral perception; only a viewer beating its Force + Spirit Energy pierces the mask.
- **Possession** *(MIS p.119; see also p.99)* — can inhabit a **willing** subject to move through the physical world without materializing. It does not take the unwilling.
- **Human Form** *(MIS p.122)* — projects its "astronaut" shape: a solid-seeming human figure in a glowing pressure suit, the form it uses to walk among people.

---

## Weakness

- **True Name** *(MIS p.117)* — like all free spirits, the Star-Captive is bound to a True Name. Anyone who learns and speaks that Name **three times in succession** compels it to appear and can attempt to bind it. It will spend — even "burn" — Spirit Energy to resist a binding *(MIS p.116)*, but a known True Name is the one real leash on it.

## GM Notes

- **Threat:** Low as a fight, high as a wild card. Its Body and Strength are negligible and it carries no attack; it never trades blows. Its danger is leverage — Possession of a willing pawn, Astral Gateway favors, and the fact that it *watches*. Treat it as a patron/omen, not a boss.
- **Motivation in play:** Guardian *(MIS p.116)* — protecting the valley's unspoiled land from corporate exploitation. It's the hook: it approaches runners only when a job aligns with derailing that kind of corp plan, and it pays in secrets, safe passage, and the occasional astral favor rather than nuyen.
- **The feather:** its calling card. A single white feather left behind means "I was here, and I'm still watching." Players will learn to read it.
- **Design:** SR3 Spirit-of-the-Sky attribute template (p.266), free-spirit Motivation + Spirit Energy + power selection per *Magic in the Shadows* (pp.116–122). The GM may swap any of the four powers for another from the Free Spirit Powers Table (MIS p.122) — powers are GM-assigned, not Force-gated. Force 3 is deliberately low; scale it up if your table needs a bigger presence.
