# THE STAR-CAPTIVE

**The Star-Captive** — Free Spirit (Guardian) · **B 1 · Q 3 · S 3 · C 1 · I 3 · W 1**

A free spirit whose form resembles a human astronaut in a slightly tattered space suit, drifting in a pale blue glow. Cracks in the helmet visor reveal starlight instead of a face, and the suit hums faintly with residual spirit energy. It seems to have been pulled from some unknown orbital craft and has been wandering the land since.

**Source:** [homebrew]

**Weakness:** The Star-Captive is susceptible to spirit attacks and has little interest in combat. It prefers observation, guidance, and gentle intervention.

**Powers:**

- **Telepathy** — The Star-Captive can communicate telepathically with any living being who does not actively resist.
- **Astral Projection** (1) — The Star-Captive can send a small portion of itself to the Astral Plane to gather information.
- **Human Form** — The Star-Captive appears as a human astronaut with a faint ethereal glow.
- **Spirit Sight** — The Star-Captive can see all spirits, spirits' true names, and their emotions.

**Spirit Energy:** The Star-Captive generates **8d6** Spirit Energy per round.

**Force:** 3 (low; a gentle guardian)

**Personality:** Shadows (observant, contemplative, drawn to the unknown)

---

**Design rationale:**

- **Guardian type** — A benevolent, observant spirit that protects/guides rather than fights. Fits the "astronaut" concept as a gentle explorer.
- **Human Form + ghostly astronaut visual** — Free Spirits can take human form; the astronaut aesthetic is a visual flavor of its materialized appearance.
- **Power set** — All four powers come from the standard Free Spirit Powers table. Telepathy and Spirit Sight emphasize its observational nature. Astral Projection (1) lets it gather information.
- **Force & personality** — Force 3 is low, matching the "Shadows" personality type (contemplative, drawn to the unknown). It's an explorer spirit, not a warrior.
