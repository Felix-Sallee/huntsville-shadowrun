# MARCUS "IRONWALL" DEVLIN — Head of Security, The Princeps Group

![Marcus Devlin](images/marcus-devlin-portrait.jpg)  
*Head of Security for The Princeps Group. Heavy cyberware and zero patience for corporate criminals.*

---

## Overview

Marcus Devlin is the iron-fisted head of security for The Princeps Group's Huntsville headquarters. A former military police officer who went deep into corporate security, he runs the physical protection detail, perimeter defense, and internal threat assessment for this miltech corporation. His cyberware — particularly his reinforced endoskeleton and ocular implants — earned him the nickname "Ironwall."

---

## What the Crew Knows

- Devlin oversees all physical security operations at Princeps's Huntsville campus
- Former military police officer before entering corporate security in 2056
- Built a reputation for brutal efficiency and zero tolerance for internal threats
- His cyberdeck is not Matrix-capable; he prefers analog surveillance and physical force
- He knows Claude August's secret identity but has signed an NDA to keep it contained
- Devlin maintains close ties with local law enforcement, using them as backup when Princeps security isn't enough

---

## Role in the Campaign

Devlin is the primary physical threat on Princeps's payroll. When runners or saboteurs try to breach Princeps facilities, they'll face his augmented guards and surveillance network first. He also runs the internal investigations that keep corporate criminals from operating within Princeps buildings — unless there's a reason for them to be let in.

## Roleplay Hooks / Secrets

- **Devlin's Secret:** He actually fears dragons and the supernatural. His military training taught him to respect power, but he gets genuinely uneasy around non-human entities. This makes him especially wary of Claude August, whom he suspects is something "off."
- **The NDA:** Devlin knows about Claude's dragon identity because a former security contractor revealed it before being killed by someone using draconic imagery (likely Princeps or CAT). He signed the NDA under threat that if anyone else finds out, his family will be hurt.
- **Loyalty Question:** If pushed hard enough, he might choose to protect his own family over keeping Claude's secret — though he'd rather die than reveal it casually.
