# ELENA ROSALES — Corporate PR Director, The Princeps Group

![Elena Rosales](images/elena-rosales-portrait.jpg)  
*Corporate PR Director for The Princeps Group. She's the only person who can talk Claude into keeping his dragon nature quiet.*

---

## Overview

Elena Rosales is the brilliant and ruthless corporate public relations director for The Princeps Group. Her job: manage Claude August's image while secretly handling the nightmare of keeping his draconic identity from becoming public knowledge. She's built a reputation as one of the best crisis communicators in Huntsville, but her real expertise is damage control when megacorps or government agencies sniff around Princeps's secret dragon CEO.

---

## What the Crew Knows

- Rosales runs all PR campaigns for Princeps and manages their media relationships
- She has direct access to Claude August and can talk him down from his draconic impulses
- Formerly worked in corporate crisis comms before joining Princeps two years ago
- Built an extensive network of journalists, politicians, and influencers who owe her favors
- Knows Claude's secret but operates under a strict NDA; threatens legal action if anyone leaks it
- She's the only person on staff who can get Claude to actually listen to reason

---

## Role in the Campaign

Rosales is crucial for any plot involving media exposure, government investigations, or megacorp scrutiny of Princeps. Runners who try to expose Claude's identity will face her counter-narratives first — she knows how to spin a story before it goes viral. She also manages the delicate balance between keeping Claude safe and not alienating him further.

## Roleplay Hooks / Secrets

- **Elena's Secret:** She actually knows how to turn Claude's dragon nature into a PR asset if necessary. If the time comes, she could spin his draconic heritage as "rare genetic engineering breakthrough" or tie it to some kind of supernatural research project that Princeps is funding for CAS.
- **The Backup Plan:** Elena has prepared multiple contingency plans for what happens if Claude's identity becomes public — from framing him as a victim of corporate persecution to exploiting megacorp xenophobia against non-humans (or dragons specifically, which is rare but possible).
- **Her Leverage:** She holds the only real leverage over Claude: she can keep his secret safe or leak it depending on what serves Princeps best. This makes her both his most loyal ally and potentially his biggest threat.
