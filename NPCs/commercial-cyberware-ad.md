## 📝 Notes for GMs

This advertisement is intended as flavor text for your Shadowrun campaign. The products mentioned are all canon items from Man & Machine: Cyberware (and other sourcebooks). Feel free to adjust prices or availability based on your campaign's economic conditions and tech levels.

**Remember:**
- All cyberware costs assume Standard grade unless otherwise specified
- Alpha/Beta/Delta grades cost 2x, 4x, and 8x the base price respectively
- Essence costs are cumulative for multiple implants of the same type
- Always check legality codes (N=No, P=Personal, S=Special, Q=Quarantine) before recommending cyberware to players
