# COLONEL (RET.) VERA KADE

![Colonel Vera Kade](images/vera-kade.jpg)  
*Colonel (Ret.) Vera Kade, Military Liaison Director and Procurement Head at Princeps.*

---

## Background

Colonel (Ret.) Vera Kade is a veteran of the CAS Army Intelligence Corps, retired on good terms with a reputation for ruthless efficiency. She handles Princeps's defense contracts, procurement, and political negotiations with military officials. Kade is the corporation's most political player — she knows exactly which politicians can protect Princeps and which ones can destroy it.

---

## What the Crew Knows

The crew knows that Colonel Kade is Princeps's political liaison to the CAS military. They know she's responsible for defense contracts and procurement, and that she has deep connections within the military establishment. Whether they know about Kade's backchannel negotiations and political maneuvering is a question for the campaign.

---

## Role in the Campaign

Colonel Kade can be used in the campaign in several ways:

- **The Client.** The crew can be hired by Kade for a military liaison contract, a procurement investigation, or a political negotiation.
- **The Witness.** The crew can witness Kade's backchannel negotiations and the political fallout of a botched procurement deal.
- **The Rival.** The crew can be hired by Kade's enemies — rival corporations, the government, or someone trying to undermine Princeps — to sabotage her work.
- **The Ally.** The crew can be recruited by Kade for a political operation, with the risk that the operation goes wrong and the crew has to clean up the fallout.

---

## Appearance and Attributes

**Age:** 56  
**Archetype:** Professional (Soldier)  
**Traits:** Calculated, authoritative, unyielding  
**Appearance:** Kade is a tall, imposing woman with a military posture and a sharp gaze. She dresses in military uniforms or tailored corporate suits, always with a sense of authority. She carries herself with the confidence of someone who has been in command for decades.
