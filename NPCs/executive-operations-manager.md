# ROBERT "CLEANER" HENDERSON — Executive Operations Manager, The Princeps Group

![Robert Henderson](images/robert-henderson-portrait.jpg)  
*Executive Operations Manager for The Princeps Group. He's the long-suffering middle manager who cleans up after Claude August's draconic messes.*

---

## Overview

Robert Henderson is the weary executive operations manager for The Princeps Group, and nobody on staff wishes he was in his position more than he does himself. His job: clean up after Claude August whenever his dragon impulses cause problems — whether that's accidental property destruction, unauthorized surveillance sweeps, or confrontations with megacorp security teams that turn into corporate crime scenes. He's earned the nickname "Cleaner" because he literally cleans up messes before they become news stories.

---

## What the Crew Knows

- Henderson manages Princeps's day-to-day operations and handles the fallout from any corporate incidents
- Former mid-level executive at a megacorp before being lured to Princeps with promises of "stability" (turns out he was just thrown into Claude's court)
- Has developed an unusual tolerance for supernatural phenomena after years of dealing with dragon-related incidents
- Knows Claude's secret but treats it as another corporate liability to be managed, not a personal revelation
- His team consists of the legal department, HR liaisons, and crisis response coordinators

---

## Role in the Campaign

Henderson is the primary NPC for any plot involving corporate fallout, cleanup operations, or aftermath scenarios. When runners cause damage at Princeps facilities, when Claude accidentally destroys property with his claws, or when megacorps investigate Princeps after a draconic incident — Henderson's team handles the paperwork and damage control. He's also the one who can get internal investigations moved quickly if needed.

## Roleplay Hooks / Secrets

- **Robert's Secret:** He actually has a supernatural sensitivity to dragons. After years of dealing with draconic incidents, he can sense when one is nearby and sometimes predict what Claude might do next — though he won't admit this to anyone for fear of being fired as "superstitious."
- **The Backup Plan:** Henderson knows that if Claude's identity becomes public knowledge, the only way to save Princeps is to frame him as a victim of corporate persecution or some kind of government experiment gone wrong. He's prepared multiple legal arguments and PR narratives for this scenario but hasn't had to use them yet.
- **His Leverage:** Henderson holds no real leverage over Claude but has developed an unusual tolerance for his boss's impulses. If pushed hard enough, he might try to help Claude contain his nature rather than just clean up the messes — though he'd never admit this openly.
