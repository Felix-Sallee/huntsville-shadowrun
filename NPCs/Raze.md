# RAZE

**Real Name:** Krag Volkov
**Archetype:** Street Samurai
**Metatype:** Ork (Male)
**Age:** 29

---

## Background

Former Ares Macrotechnology corporate security operator, Seattle division. Three years ago, an extraction job went sideways in the Redmond Barrens. His team got pinned down and corporate pulled the plug — no extraction, no backup, no acknowledgment they existed. Krag crawled out of the wreckage, buried two teammates, and burned his SIN. The corp called it a "training accident." He calls it murder.

Now he works the shadows under the name Raze. He's methodical, professional, and carries enough chrome and firepower to remind anyone who crosses him exactly why Ares trained him in the first place. He lives by a simple code: protect your team, finish the job, and never trust a suit.

## Notes
