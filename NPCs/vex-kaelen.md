# Vex "Razor" Kaelen

> _"I don't run from the shadows, I dance in them."_ — Vex Kaelen

**Vex "Razor" Kaelen** is a mid-level Orc Ganger runner who rose through the ranks of the Lower Sprawl before earning respect across multiple crews. A natural negotiator with an eye for opportunity, Vex specializes in information brokering and small-scale heists that leverage insider knowledge rather than brute force.

## Background

Born into the crushing poverty of Sector 13, Vex learned early that survival required more than just grit — it demanded intelligence, adaptability, and the ability to read people before they could react. They started as a courier for various gangs, quickly outgrew their station through cunning and charm, and eventually found themselves at the mercy of the Orcs' recruitment drive when an Orc contact recognized Vex's potential.

Despite being metahuman, Vex was drawn to the Orcs' philosophy: _"We don't care what you are, only who you are."_ They embraced the gang's culture while maintaining their own identity as a pragmatic operator who gets results.

Vex maintains careful ties with several other gangs (especially the Goblins and Chimeras) but always prioritizes the Orcs' interests when jobs conflict. Their reputation for keeping word to themselves has made them a valuable asset for information-sensitive operations.
