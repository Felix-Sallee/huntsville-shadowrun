---

## Background
Claude August is the young CEO and controlling owner of [The Princeps Group](Princeps-Group.html), a Huntsville-based CAS miltech corporation. He emerged as Princeps's new owner after the previous leadership became embroiled in a financial scandal, executing a government-backed stock takeover that placed him in control of the corporation.

The players know Claude is secretly a juvenile western dragon — a fact he keeps hidden because the Confederated American States may not recognize him as a citizen if his draconic nature is exposed. If CAS does not recognize Claude's citizenship, his legal ownership of Princeps could be challenged or voided by corporate rivals and political opponents.

---

## Role in the Campaign
Claude serves as:
- Corporate principal behind a protected CAS miltech research company
- Legal and political pressure point where corporate ownership, national security, and dragon identity collide
- A living reason Princeps's independence is fragile: the same secret that makes Claude extraordinary could imperil his ownership
- Current suspect behind the Pixel Sticks proxy attack on Cross Applied Technology interests (pending stronger evidence)

- Control of an independent single-A miltech research corporation
- Political backing from CAS interests that want Princeps to remain a domestic counterweight to megacorp influence

## Vulnerabilities
