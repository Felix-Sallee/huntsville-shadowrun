Always commit and push changes to the huntsville-shadowrun wiki repo whenever new information is added or updated. The wiki at felix-sallee.github.io/huntsville-shadowrun is the primary player-facing resource.

**Why:** Players view the wiki directly; local-only changes are invisible to them. Visibility to players is the primary concern unless Daniel specifies otherwise.

**How to apply:** After any edit to files in `huntsville-shadowrun/`, stage, commit, and push to `origin main` before reporting the task done.
