## Overview

**The Princeps Group** is an independent single-A corporation headquartered in Huntsville, in the Confederated American States. Princeps is a military-technology research corporation with unusually strong ties to the CAS military, protected by the CAS government as a domestic counterweight to larger international megacorporate influence.

Princeps specializes in advanced military hardware, cyberwarfare systems, biotech defense applications, and next-generation weapons platforms. The corporation's research operations are conducted in multiple black sites across the US, with the main Huntsville campus serving as the public-facing hub.

The corporation was founded in the wake of the 2020s megacorp consolidation wave, created as a government-backed domestic miltech firm to keep critical defense research within US control. The current leadership — under CEO Claude August — operates as a government proxy with extraordinary autonomy.

## Leadership

### Claude August

- **Role:** Chief Executive Officer
- **Age:** Appears to be in his teens
- **Archetype:** Decker (CEO / Corporate Principal)

Claude August is the young, seemingly inexperienced CEO of the Princeps Group. Publicly, he's a corporate prodigy who inherited leadership from a scandal-ridden predecessor. Privately, he's a juvenile Western dragon in human disguise, with his true nature protected by a network of NDAs, legal loopholes, and military-grade security.

**The crew knows about Claude's secret identity.** Whether they're the only ones who does is a running question in the campaign.

## What the Crew Knows

The Princeps crew is well-versed in the corporation's structure:

- **The NDA.** Everyone on Princeps knows that CEO Claude August's true nature is a closely guarded secret. The secret is protected by a combination of NDAs, legal loopholes, and military-grade security. The crew knows who knows, who doesn't, and where the weak points are.

- **The Black Sites.** The crew knows about the deniable research facilities off the main campus, including Black Site 4, which is the primary site for Aris Thorne's unapproved research. The crew knows that these sites have their own security detail and that access is tightly controlled.

- **The Government Backing.** The crew knows that the CAS government protects Princeps as a domestic counterweight to international megacorp influence. This means that any attack on Princeps is effectively an attack on the government, and the response will be swift and brutal.

- **The Pixel Sticks.** The crew knows that Princeps is suspected of sponsoring the Pixel Sticks proxy attacks on CAT subsidiaries. This means that the corporation is actively engaged in cyber warfare and that the crew may be implicated in future operations.

- **The Internal Politics.** The crew knows that there are tensions within Princeps between the scientific leadership (Aris), the political leadership (Kade), and the security leadership (Devlin). Each faction has their own priorities and methods, and the crew can exploit these divisions.

## Role in the Campaign

The Princeps Group can be used in the campaign in several ways:

### As a Client
The crew can be hired by Princeps for various contracts — security audits, corporate espionage investigations, political negotiations, or research protection. These contracts can lead to deeper involvement in the corporation's affairs.

### As a Rival
Princeps can be a direct rival to the crew's employers, especially Cross Applied Technology. The crew can be tasked with gathering information on Princeps, sabotaging their operations, or even assassinating key personnel.

### As a Neutral Party
Princeps can be a neutral party in larger conflicts, with the crew navigating the political landscape to maintain relationships on both sides. This can be a delicate balancing act, especially if the crew is asked to take sides.

### As a Threat
Princeps can be a threat to the crew if the corporation discovers the crew's involvement in operations that could compromise the corporation's secrets or reputation. The crew can be investigated, blacklisted, or even eliminated by the corporation's security apparatus.

## Key Locations

### The Princeps Campus (Huntsville HQ)

The main Princeps campus is a sprawling corporate complex in the Huntsville business district. The campus includes:

- **The Executive Tower:** Claude August's office and the main conference facilities.
- **The R&D Wings:** The research laboratories and testing facilities.
- **The Security Center:** Devlin's operations base and the guard headquarters.
- **The Data Vault:** Chloe Okafor's network infrastructure and server rooms.
- **The Briefing Room:** A secure conference room for internal meetings.
- **The Mess Hall:** The employee cafeteria and social hub.

### Black Site 4

Black Site 4 is a deniable research facility off the main campus, accessible only by private transport. The site is run by Aris Thorne and houses his most unapproved research projects. The security is tight, but not impenetrable — a crew with the right skills and information could infiltrate the site.

### The Vault

The Vault is a secure data center within the main campus, managed by Chloe Okafor. All of Princeps's proprietary data, research, and financial records are stored here. The Vault is heavily guarded and monitored, but Chloe has backdoor access that the rest of the security team doesn't know about.

### The Briefing Room

The Briefing Room is a secure conference facility within the main campus, used for internal meetings and briefings. The room is equipped with encrypted displays, secure comms, and biometric entry. It's a key location for corporate decision-making and can be a valuable target for espionage.

### The Mess Hall

The Mess Hall is the employee cafeteria and social hub within the main campus. It's a place where gossip spreads fast, and employees vent about their bosses. The mess hall is a social risk rather than a security risk, but it's a valuable location for gathering information.

## Security

The Princeps Group security apparatus is rated **Level 3** — standard corporate security with military-grade augmentation for key personnel. The security measures include:

- **Perimeter Sensors:** Motion sensors, drone surveillance, and thermal imaging around the campus.
- **Biometric Entry Points:** Facial recognition, retinal scans, and DNA verification for all entry points.
- **Internal Surveillance:** Cameras, wiretaps, and data monitoring throughout the campus.
- **Private Guard Detail:** Devlin's team of 15 augmented guards and 5 surveillance techs.
- **Black Site Security:** A separate security detail for Black Site 4, run by Aris Thorne.

The security is tight, but not impenetrable. A crew with the right skills and information can bypass most of the security measures. However, the crew should be aware that there are always more security layers than they can see.

## Political Background

The Princeps Group was founded in the wake of the 2020s megacorp consolidation wave, created as a government-backed domestic miltech firm to keep critical defense research within US control. The corporation was designed to be an independent single-A entity with strong CAS military ties.

The previous Princeps leadership was involved in a financial scandal — embezzlement, possibly corporate espionage. The government-backed takeover was a clean slate. Claude August, the new CEO, was a surprise. The previous leadership thought he was a regular corporate consultant; he turned out to be a government-backed proxy with draconic biology.

The government's interest in Princeps: a domestic counterweight to foreign megacorp influence. If Princeps falls, the CAS loses a key position in miltech research, and foreign corporations will move in to fill the gap.

## Current Operations

### The Pixel Sticks Frame

Princeps is currently under investigation for suspected sponsorship of the Pixel Sticks proxy attacks on CAT subsidiaries. The corporation denies the allegations, but the evidence is mounting. The crew can be hired to investigate the frame, help Princeps clean its name, or exploit the investigation to gather information on Princeps's operations.

### The Dragon Citizenship Loophole

Princeps is pursuing a legal maneuver to formalize Claude August's ownership while keeping his identity public but legally protected. The maneuver involves a special citizenship classification that would allow a non-human entity to own and run a corporation. This could be a major plot thread if the crew wants to leverage it or exploit it.

### Dr. Thorne's Project

Aris Thorne is running a secret research project on Black Site 4 that could change the geopolitical landscape. The project's nature is unknown to most of the corporation, including the security leadership. The crew can discover the project, help Thorne with the research, or try to shut it down.

### Project Ironclad

Devlin's private militia, known as "Project Ironclad," handles black-site security and deniable operations. The existence of Project Ironclad is unknown to most of the corporation, including the security leadership. The crew can discover the project, be recruited as a member, or witness its operations firsthand.

## Factions

### Cross Applied Technology (CAT)

**Relationship:** Active Rival

CAT is investigating Princeps's connection to the Pixel Sticks proxy attacks. Tensions are escalating, and the two corporations are engaged in a cold war of corporate espionage and political maneuvering. The crew can be hired by CAT to investigate Princeps, sabotage Princeps's operations, or even assassinate key Princeps personnel.

### Ares Technologies

**Relationship:** Strategic Competitor

Ares Technologies sees Princeps as a threat to its domestic miltech market and has been looking for ways to undercut or acquire Princeps. The two corporations have a competitive relationship, with occasional attempts at corporate espionage and sabotage. The crew can be hired by Ares to compete against Princeps, sabotage Princeps's operations, or help Ares acquire Princeps.

### Saeder-Krupp

**Relationship:** Neutral Competitor

Saeder-Krupp has business relationships with Princeps but watches the corporation closely for opportunities to strike. The two corporations have a professional relationship, but Saeder-Krupp is always looking for a way to get an edge over Princeps. The crew can be hired by Saeder-Krupp to compete against Princeps, gather information on Princeps's operations, or even sabotage Princeps's reputation.

### Wuxing

**Relationship:** Interest Only

Wuxing wants Princeps's miltech technology but doesn't have the CAS political connections to buy it. Wuxing has been looking for ways to acquire Princeps's technology, but the political barriers are too high. The crew can be hired by Wuxing to acquire Princeps's technology, sabotage Princeps's operations, or even assassinate key Princeps personnel.

## Campaign Hooks

### The Frame Job

The crew is hired to investigate the Pixel Sticks frame job and either prove Princeps's innocence or expose the frame. Either way, the investigation leads to deeper involvement in Princeps's operations and the broader CAT-Princeps cold war.

### The Dragon Loophole

The crew is hired to help Princeps formalize Claude August's ownership through the Dragon Citizenship Loophole. The process involves navigating the legal and political landscape, dealing with opposition from rival corporations, and protecting the secret about Claude's identity.

### The Black Site

The crew is hired to infiltrate Black Site 4 and discover Aris Thorne's secret project. The project could be a breakthrough in miltech or a threat to the nation's security — it all depends on what the crew discovers.

### The Internal Probe

The crew is hired to investigate an internal leak within Princeps. The leak could be a corporate spy, a government mole, or someone trying to protect the secret about Claude's identity. The investigation leads to deeper involvement in Princeps's internal politics and security.

### The Cleanup

The crew is hired to investigate one of Robert "Cleaner" Henderson's cleanup operations. The operation could go wrong, and the crew could be drawn into a crisis that threatens the corporation's reputation and security.

## Notes for the GM

The Princeps Group is designed to be a flexible faction that can be used in a variety of ways. The corporation is powerful but not invincible, with internal politics, security vulnerabilities, and political pressures that can all be exploited by the crew.

The secret about Claude August's dragon identity is a central thread in the Princeps storyline. How the crew handles this secret — whether they protect it, exploit it, or try to expose it — will shape their relationship with the corporation and the broader political landscape.

The crew can interact with the Princeps crew in a variety of ways — as clients, rivals, or neutral parties. The key is to keep the relationships fluid and dynamic, with the corporation's internal politics and external pressures shaping the interactions.
